# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project — issues, pull requests, reproduction reports,
and discussion — to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

* being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences;
* giving and gracefully accepting constructive criticism;
* focusing on what is best for the community and for the science;
* showing empathy toward other participants.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

* sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances;
* trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
* public or private harassment;
* publishing others' private information without explicit permission;
* other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## A note specific to this project

This repository exists so that published results can be checked. **Disagreement with
the paper's findings is welcome and is not a conduct issue** — evidence-based
criticism is the point of publishing an artifact. What is not welcome is hostility
toward the people involved.

If you believe a claim here is wrong, the most useful thing you can do is show your
work: which artifact, which digest, which command, what you got.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported
to the corresponding author at **bellayang@anl.gov**. All complaints will be reviewed
and investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy will be respected.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may face
repercussions as determined by other members of the project's leadership.
